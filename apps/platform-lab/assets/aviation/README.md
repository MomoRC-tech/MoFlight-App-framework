Scene: calm realistic small European grass airfield with two white fiberglass gliders and one small light aircraft parked on the ground, plus a clearly recognizable orange-and-white windsock on a pole. All aircraft are firmly stationary, wings and landing gear resting naturally; no flying aircraft. Composition is critical: arrange the grounded aircraft, a modest hangar and windsock along a narrow horizontal band across the CENTER of the RIGHT 60 percent, so they remain recognizable when cropped into a very shallow header. The windsock should be modest in height and its orange fabric should read near the middle height. LEFT 45 percent mostly quiet very dark navy #14232f negative space with only a hint of grass/atmospheric gradient for separately rendered light title text. Muted blue-hour light, cool navy/slate shading, restrained mint/cyan reflections and a small warm windsock accent to match an understated dark aviation dashboard. Photograph-like material detail and plausible glider proportions; softly simplified distant background. No dramatic sunset, no neon, no borders, no frame, no typography, no watermark, no specific club or geographical landmarks. Decorative fictional airfield, not real club identity or live weather. Keep the main subjects compact in the central horizontal strip and the overall artwork understated enough for a small 28-40dp-high title background.
