### Exact values, dates and revisions

- **Money:** only explicit source currency `EUR` with two minor-unit places. Accept outer whitespace, optional ASCII minus, ungrouped digits or correctly grouped German thousands dots, and one comma followed by exactly two digits. Examples below are synthetic. Reject plus/Unicode minus, currency suffixes, parentheses, internal spaces, exponent notation, locale guessing and rounding. Blank is missing; numeric zero is known zero. Unsupported currency remains separate/unavailable, with no FX. Preserve raw text regardless of parse result. The [isolated parser](../../apps/platform-lab/src/vereinsflieger/expense-money-observation.ts) bounds input to 128 characters, parses with `BigInt` and checks safe integer cents before conversion. E2 must also check each arithmetic operation; individually safe inputs do not make a sum safe.
- **Signs/kinds:** preserve raw movement sign. Expense sign is a separate validated mapping: personal debit charge → positive incurred cost; explicitly linked charge credit/refund → negative incurred cost; payment/top-up and payment reversal → account movement only. A positive movement alone establishes neither a refund nor a payment; unknown kind/liability remains an unresolved movement, excluded from expense totals with an unavailable result. Opening/running balance never enters arithmetic for incurred costs. Do not derive expense from balance differences.
- **Period basis:** use **ledger booking date (`Buchungsdatum`, source `Datum`)**, inclusive calendar-date bounds, for the first ledger-based view. Validate actual `dd.MM.yyyy` dates in the existing 1900–2199 reader range; keep ISO calendar dates without timezone conversion. A flight/service date or invoice date is preserved separately and never silently substitutes. A later refund belongs to its own booking period and retains its original-charge relation. If ledger date meaning cannot be confirmed on the next source pass, booking-period expense output stays unavailable. UI must label this basis before E3 acceptance; no separate product decision is needed unless source evidence requires changing it.
- **Observation versus business identity:** keep capture ID/hash/time + row position as evidence addresses only. Booking/invoice numbers are opaque source strings until uniqueness, owner/club/account scope and reuse/revision behavior are proved. Identical same-key/same-revision observations deduplicate only after that proof. A higher **proved source revision** may supersede; a later capture timestamp alone cannot. Changed values under one revision, missing keys, simultaneous variants or incompatible links remain conflicts; retain originals. Reversals are explicit new movements with relations, not inferred deletion. Missing rows in a later partial page are not deletions.
- **Balances:** expose individual raw dated balances with their source capture, sequence and context. Same-date rows may be ordered only by evidenced source sequence. Do not choose a period closing balance from the maximum date or sum balances across rows/captures/clubs. Complete balance reconciliation requires an evidenced ordered interval and its opening state, which current evidence lacks.

## Calculation rules to freeze with source evidence

- Count expenses once. A flight cost, invoice and ledger posting may describe the same charge; select one authoritative representation or prove explicit reconciliation before combining them.
- Do not transfer the flight-count rule `Duplikat: Ja` blindly to money. A financial charge needs its own identity, reversal and liability evidence; a duplicate-looking flight neither proves nor disproves a payable charge.
- Separate gross incurred cost, payments/credits and balance. Refund treatment follows proved source kind/sign; a top-up is not a negative flying expense.
- Group by explicit currency and club/account. No currency conversion, inferred cross-club balance or date-based aircraft allocation. Unknown aircraft/category remains explicit.
- Period membership uses one named source date basis. Do not mix flight/service dates and ledger posting dates silently. Compare like coverage; a partial year is not a complete annual expense.
- No automatic classification from ambiguous free text. Use evidenced source categories or a reviewed deterministic mapping; ambiguous rows stay unclassified. AI does not calculate or classify this version.
