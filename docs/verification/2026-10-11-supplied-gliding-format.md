This supersedes the request to choose his first real book. No publisher or edition is inferred from the earlier representative supplier samples. The photograph remains in the private user attachment, outside Git.

The spread has ten entry rows. Page carryover, page sum and total are additional destinations; this first draft does not implement their calculation or populate signature fields.
