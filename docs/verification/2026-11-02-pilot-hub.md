## Blue palette, less copy and combined metrics — owner-requested revision

The owner rejected the first colors/clumsy appearance and requested substantially less text and richer combined metric visualization. Current source uses deep aviation blue/sky actions, borderless tonal surfaces, compact flight/club/cost rows and paired paper/assistant entries. The unfinished paper workflow no longer dominates Start. Paper, Club and Assistant entries omit repeated marketing/help paragraphs; source/error/unavailable distinctions remain.

`PilotSeasonInstrument` combines current known flight time, aircraft-time sections, matching labelled mini-bars, current flight count and a previous-year count marker. Aircraft shares reuse `summarizeRealFlights` and `seasonRingSlices`; differences reuse `seasonDifference`. Inner flight-count arcs are scaled to the larger saved annual count, not a target; outer sections are known-time distributions. Unknown/zero values create no false progress; incomplete dates/durations remain identified. Cached summaries do not invent aircraft breakdowns. Complete aircraft details are reachable directly. Native strip rendering reuses the established technique without new dependencies; no runtime performance claim is made.

Current focused evidence: TypeScript and changed-file ESLint pass; 67/67 tests in `pilot-hub.test.ts` plus `mobile-header-navigation.test.ts` pass, including two new known/unknown/zero instrument checks. Logs: `build/pilot-ui-review/visual-revision-types.log` and `visual-revision-tests.log`. Earlier 26 tests retain their original-source limits; no full suite or device build was repeated.
