## Scope and meaning of “mandatory”

This reviews the implemented runtime verification families reached by ordinary combined Sync and its shared local admission, selection, recovery, save and readback paths, including the other-source jobs, advisory preview/status and Development hooks. Individual field assertions are grouped under their actual validator; this is not a claim to audit every assertion in unrelated AI/cloud/photo/book/financial-development flows or to delete development tests. Native backup/restore admission is reviewed where it blocks Sync; whole-archive import verification remains a separate operation.

“Contract-required” means necessary to an existing ownership, completeness, preservation or recovery property. It is not a claim that a law requires this exact algorithm or frequency. Full-history replay on every open, exact-schema comparisons at every read and repeated profile navigation are implementation choices, not immutable requirements. Any replacement still has to preserve the required property and show its actual benefit. Risk below describes deleting the check without a replacement.
