## Recommendation

Use an **offline-first Help layer** with two depths. A small contextual bubble explains an unfamiliar term or state at the point of use. **Hilfe & Einführung**, opened from Profile → Support or a bubble's **Mehr erfahren**, provides searchable, maintained articles and short question/answer bubbles. The latter are visibly labelled **Hilfe · Offline**, not presented as a live chat. Keep the existing AI tab for explicit AI conversations. An optional **Im AI-Chat weiterfragen** link can be considered later, after source, privacy, request-limit and funding decisions; it must never auto-send the Help question or flight context.

This serves three distinct jobs without a permanent floating button over flight controls: explain the current screen, recover a known task, and find a term. It also gives dismissed first-use hints a stable place to be reopened. No new bottom tab is proposed.

| Option | Usefulness and upkeep | Privacy, offline and operating cost | Recommendation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Long documentation alone | Broad reference but weak at the moment of confusion; content still needs review | Offline if bundled; no per-question service fee | Keep as deep reference, not the only entry |
| Curated glossary/FAQ with contextual bubbles | Fast answers and deterministic links to the affected screen; small but real editorial/versioning work | Bundled text works offline and needs no flight upload or per-question service fee | **First implementation slice** |
| AI-only help | Flexible follow-up wording, but source quality and context need evaluation; service and abuse controls add work | Unavailable offline; personal context can be disclosed if sent; variable paid usage | Do not use as the default Help surface |
| Offline Help plus optional AI handoff | Deterministic common answers plus explicit deeper conversation | Offline fallback remains; AI request needs separate consent/admission, cap and review | Possible later slice, not selected here |

## Intended UI and interaction

1. **Context bubble.** A labelled **Info zu PIC** target sits with the displayed role, with a 48 dp touch area. Tap opens one short bubble, anchored below the field when there is room. On a narrow screen, large text, keyboard overlap or insufficient safe area, use the same content in a bottom sheet. It has a title, at most a short paragraph, **Mehr erfahren**, and Close. The bubble never covers the term or its primary action and does not open on hover alone. Only one is open at a time.
2. **Optional first-use bubble.** One relevant hint may appear after arriving at an area, but never as a mandatory tour or above an active login/retrieval/recovery overlay. It says what the area contains and offers **Verstanden**, **Später**, and **Hilfe öffnen**; **Alle Hinweise überspringen** is available in the introduction. Dismissal is not acknowledgment of new flight content. Opening or replaying hints performs no provider, backup or writing operation.
3. **Help sheet.** Profile → Support & Über MoFlight → **Hilfe & Einführung** opens a full-height app sheet with contextual Back, a visible **Hilfe · Offline** label, search, and categories **Erste Schritte**, **Flüge & Daten**, **Flugbuch**, **Begriffe**, **Sicherung & Datenschutz**. It is capped and centered on wide screens, scrollable at 360 dp/large text, and returns to the exact prior route and scroll/filter state. Search finds titles, approved synonyms and short body text; no network is required.
4. **Answer bubbles.** Opening a suggested question such as **Was bedeutet PIC?** shows a distinct question bubble and a larger answer bubble. The answer includes plain-language explanation, source/version, a short **Für diesen Flug** boundary and **Zurück zum Flug**. Suggested next questions are buttons, not generated predictions. No fake typing indicator, avatar or composer: these are curated Help entries. A missing answer says **Dazu haben wir noch keine geprüfte Hilfe** and offers category browsing or feedback through a later approved support route, without guessing.
5. **AI boundary.** The existing AI tab retains its own message bubbles, availability notice, explicit Send and service/privacy disclosure. If a future handoff is selected, it opens an unsent local draft with the user's question only; flight identifiers, records, page state and source snippets need explicit scoped inclusion. Offline, disabled and budget-exhausted states keep the offline answer available. Help never changes a saved role, credit, Medical status, book or source data.

### Representative answer and source boundary

**Question:** “Was bedeutet PIC?”  **Short bubble:** “PIC steht für Pilot-in-command: die für diesen Flug verantwortliche Pilotin oder der verantwortliche Pilot. Mehr erfahren.”

**Full Help answer:** “PIC bezeichnet die für die sichere Durchführung des Fluges bestimmte verantwortliche Pilotin oder den verantwortlichen Piloten. In MoFlight zeigt das Kürzel zunächst die gespeicherte Quellenangabe. Ob und wie Zeit in einem konkreten Flugbuch angerechnet wird, folgt daraus nicht automatisch.” The definition is grounded in the [EU pilot-in-command definition (EUR-Lex, Regulation 1178/2011)](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2011/1178/2022-10-30/eng) and should be rechecked against the applicable current source before publishing content. The second sentence states the existing MoFlight raw-evidence/deterministic-credit boundary, not a legal credit judgment.

Other initial question candidates: **Warum sehe ich keine Flüge?** (show locally known account/receipt/empty state and link to existing Datenstatus or explicit Sync; never infer provider completeness), **Was bedeutet „teilweise aktualisiert“?** (identify completed and failed independent jobs), **Was wird gesichert?** (describe only the supported text-data archive and its exclusions; no attachment/non-text promise), and **Wo finde ich meine Flugdetails?** (route to Flüge). Final wording needs an editorial pass against the implemented state and backup contract.

## Content and state contract for a selected implementation

- Bundle a small versioned German text catalogue with stable entry IDs, category, title, search terms, short/full answer, app source or authoritative external source URL, reviewed date and reviewer. App-operation answers are reviewed by the feature owner when behavior changes; aviation/legal terms require a named domain reviewer and periodic source check. Mark uncertainty and scope in the answer rather than hiding it in a global disclaimer. External links are optional and never required to read the bundled answer offline.
- Generic dismissal/replay preference is local to the installation and content version. Account-dependent hints are keyed to the current account and recomputed after a switch; book-specific guidance is keyed to the book. Opening Help must not read another account's cached data. A catalogue update may re-show only materially changed guidance, without resetting new-content markers.
- Accessibility: visible text labels, 48 dp targets, logical focus into/out of sheet, Close/Back semantics, screen-reader announcement of bubble title and source, no colour-only state, no motion requirement. Onboarding hints remain user-triggered or restrained, dismissible and never trap focus. Validate 360 dp, 390–412 dp, 600+ dp, large font, keyboard, Android Back and offline return in native acceptance.
- If an AI extension is chosen later, define approved sources and answer/evidence evaluation, minimized context, explicit Send, per-account request and abuse limits, finite pre-funded cap, cutoff and fallback. Recheck official service prices and the #13/#21/#28 decisions before activation. This proposal authorizes no model call, service change, upload or spending.

## Selection and delivery boundary

The 25 October implementation instruction selects the offline-first two-depth pattern and curated question/answer **bubbles**. The first source slice bundles PIC, empty flights, partial update, backup scope, flight details and current Flugbuch status. It adds Profile Help/search, local contextual links, one dismissible populated-Flüge hint with installation-local dismissal/replay, and existing overlay Back/return. The implementation uses an inline PIC bubble within ordinary Flüge details so it never covers facts; the earlier sketch's more free-floating anchor is not a native geometry specification. The existing Statistics contributor detail currently reuses the source presentation without the extra PIC Help action; its return stack remains undisturbed. Any unified Statistics entry or AI handoff is follow-up work. Native layout/focus/keyboard checks and named aviation-content reviewer remain open under #31. The [visual sketch](design/proposals/2026-10-25-help-system.html) is browser-only and synthetic.
