## First use — guided start and trust

**Added 18 October 2026, requested by Manuel; tracking: [#22](https://github.com/MomoRC-tech/MoFlight-App-framework/issues/22).** This complements the seven areas reviewed on 13 October; that earlier review does not approve this addition's detailed design. **Requested outcome:** after downloading and first opening MoFlight, the user understands what it helps with, receives guidance around first login and data security, knows a useful first action, sees which Start tiles gain information after retrieval, and learns what each area shows and can do when first opening it. Manuel suggests Vereinsflieger login as the first action; this is the proposed default below, with a route to explore or return later.

**Refined UX proposal:** one short welcome followed by help where the user needs it. Prefer inline cards and an optional anchored hint beside a relevant control; use an app-owned sheet for the pre-login explanation. Show one hint at a time, with a clear dismiss/skip action. Avoid a mandatory tour of every feature. Instructions must stay available through help after dismissal. These are reviewable design recommendations, not selected components or implemented behavior. Keep the approved visual reference and existing navigation; guidance supplements them.

### Paper-total adjustments

**Accepted product rule, Manuel, 18 October 2026 ([#7](https://github.com/MomoRC-tech/MoFlight-App-framework/issues/7)):** the pilot may explicitly accept a historical totals discrepancy and continue from a known, user-confirmed paper-book total. Future writing templates add new authoritative Vereinsflieger flight durations to that accepted paper carryover. Persist the difference as a **book-specific totals adjustment**, rather than leaving it only in a dismissed warning or the current template. This accepts continuation arithmetic and persistent adjustment behavior; it does not authorize implementation, a physical schema or release acceptance.

Manuel selected on 18 October 2026: **review all affected totals together and confirm once**. Show each total's paper value, provider comparison and difference in that review. One explicit confirmation approves the displayed scoped set, with a separate adjustment for each affected total linked to that confirmation. It does not approve unseen/future mismatches or another book. Exact layout/copy remains open.

First verify the photo transcription and compare the same book/flight boundary, time category and available source coverage. Show paper total, provider comparison and difference; incomplete history or unreadable evidence must not become an invented discrepancy. For a verified comparison at anchor A, store accepted paper total C, provider total P and signed adjustment D = C − P in integer minutes. Future paper total = C + the durations of the new flights included after A. Equivalently, P + D + those new durations; apply D once, not once per page, export or reopening. A later confirmed paper carryover already containing D must not receive D again. Example: paper 100:00 versus provider 99:40 gives D = +0:20; a new 0:30 flight yields paper 100:30 versus provider 100:10. Negative differences remain possible; an unavailable provider comparison/difference stays unknown.

Persist account/book, anchor/entry, affected time-total category, accepted paper value, provider comparison/coverage/revision, signed difference when known, supporting photo/source/confirmation references, pilot/date and adjustment revision/status. New writing snapshots reference the adjustment revision they used. Preserve it across offline restart, future batches and backup/restore; keep other books/accounts isolated. A known accepted carryover can be stored while an unavailable comparison remains unknown, without fabricating an offset. Exact supported category mappings remain format-specific contract work.

Mark the discrepancy **accepted/left unchanged**, not corrected. Preserve original evidence, provider facts, independent provider-based app statistics and frozen writing snapshots; do not create a fictitious flight to hold the difference. Reuse the unchanged scoped decision without repeated questions. Retain reversal/supersession history. Manuel selected on 18 October 2026: when a later verified source correction affects an already-written flight, offer to leave its paper entry/totals unchanged and approve an updated totals adjustment. Show the affected totals together for one confirmation; keep authoritative provider values/app statistics current. Recompute the proposed difference against the same confirmed paper carryover and aligned revised provider comparison, not by adding the old adjustment again. Preserve prior decisions/snapshots; do not silently change the accepted carryover or require retrospective paper correction. Exact concurrent-update handling remains open. This is separate from acknowledging an orange source-update marker, whose comparison covers changes since the last acknowledgement. Exact action/copy, category mappings and later correction workflow remain to be refined.

**Persistent deviation visibility, Manuel, 18 October 2026 ([#30](https://github.com/MomoRC-tech/MoFlight-App-framework/issues/30)):** flights with an evidenced paper/source deviation need a dot or small label in flight lists as well as a visible status in details, including after the pilot accepts leaving the paper unchanged. Keep source-update attention and deviation status independent: acknowledging a source update can clear its orange attention indication, but must not hide an ongoing accepted deviation.
