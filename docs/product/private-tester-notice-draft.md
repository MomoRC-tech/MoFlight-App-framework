## Minimum applicability boundary

Keep the unresolved role/commercial-context question narrow: supplying local software does not alone settle who controls each processing operation. Limited invitations alone do not establish the GDPR personal/household exemption. Where a notice is required, contact and accurate processing information matter; a concise layered presentation is supported by the [EDPB transparency guidance](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/sme/be-compliant/respect-individuals-rights_en) and [role explanation](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/sme/learn-the-basics/data-controller-or-data-processor_en).

For an Impressum, [DDG §5](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/ddg/__5.html) concerns the specified businesslike services, and [MStV §18](https://www.gesetze-bayern.de/Content/Document/MStV-18) has a separate personal/family-purpose boundary. Personal operation in Germany is not enough to declare either applicable or exempt. Confirm the actual noncommercial/personal versus organized/commercial context before demanding or publishing additional fields. No separate editorial-responsible person, company/register/tax ID or new legal surface is assumed. This is the bounded remaining applicability question under #98, not a general compliance programme.
