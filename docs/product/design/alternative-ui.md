# Alternative UI — 22 October 2026

[Handbook](README.md) · [#55](https://github.com/MomoRC-tech/MoFlight-App-framework/issues/55) · branch `ui/alternative-flight-experience`.

Manuel authorized an alternative native implementation and verification. Choosing it as the main product design remains a separate decision. M4 presentation reuses existing M2/M3 saved-data and provider boundaries. This visual contract adds no services, migrations or real-book activation. Provider retrieval changes are tracked separately and do not follow from appearance approval.

## Review and design response

The baseline makes too many elements equally prominent: tiny header title beside three status controls, gradient cards around every group, dense flight metadata before results, and charts mixed with everyday tasks. The alternative establishes a reading order: destination → essential actions → flight information → optional detail.

- **Foundation:** matte navy canvas `#0b141c`, opaque panels `#14232f`, raised controls `#1d3242`, high-contrast off-white text, muted blue-grey labels and mint action color `#76dfc6`. Preserve the selected duotone art and Thermik logo. No external fonts or image generation.
- **Typography and spacing:** 15/22 body, 19/26 section headings, 28/34 header title and 18/24 ring-adjacent season values. Shared cards use 18-unit padding and 22-unit corners; the dense season card uses 14-unit padding. Page spacing is 18. Primary actions are at least 52 units, labelled icon targets at least 48. Text follows system scaling.
- **Header (C05/C06):** decorative flight-scene artwork over navy, 46-unit unchanged Thermik logo, destination title and labelled Data/Medical/Profile controls. Phones place controls below the destination; wide layouts share one row and enlarged text wraps. Header-only update/login admission remains unchanged.
- **Start — revised after Manuel's feedback:** remove the permanent leading connection/reminder box. Meine Saison is the first content group, with a small flight illustration and one latest saved, dated flight integrated into the same card. Prioritize calendar date/relative age (for example −4 Tage or −2 Wochen), recorded PIC and aircraft. Do not infer PIC from another crew slot. Its row opens the exact saved flight; Alle Flüge ansehen opens the general list. Unknown dates/times and ambiguous same-day chronology remain explicit.
- **Season comparison:** four compact aircraft-split rings: known reported time and starts for each of the current/prior calendar years. Aircraft colors stay stable across measures/years and have a shared registration legend. Empty/unknown time has no fabricated arc. Ring values sit beside the artwork so enlarged text can grow; the measure pair stacks at enlarged text. These are saved-year totals, not a claim of complete coverage or equal year-to-date periods.
- **Saison im Detail:** opens directly onto one dense current/prior-year monthly chart with Flüge/Flugstunden chips, without a duplicate aircraft list. Jan–Dec always occupy one row; all twelve months fit visibly without horizontal scrolling, including enlarged text. Only the compact month/marker labels cap font scaling at 1; surrounding controls and captions still scale. Preserve year labels, unknown/partial markers, equal current-month cutoffs and blank future months. Expansion performs no provider work.
- **Flüge (C04/C09):** prominent search, year chips, aircraft filter, concise saved-row count, optional coverage disclosure. Clear search without clearing the year, or reset all filters with one action. The list shows date, reported time, registration, model and route. Details lead with aircraft and duration, with complete original evidence below. No inferred route, track, role or flight time.
- **Navigation (C07):** five preserved destinations, selected filled tile and heavier mint label. Enlarged text uses wrapped rows. The development footer names this as an alternative.
- **Empty/deferred states:** explain the next supported step. Paper setup stays “In Vorbereitung”; AI explains its synthetic example scope. A compact Meine Saison empty/error state gives passive guidance to the header Daten action without invented totals or the removed permanent connection box.
- **Responsive composition:** bounded single groups through phone/tablet widths; at 840+ a two-column Start. Flight rows use two columns at 600+ with standard font scale. The month canvas remains a single row at every width; all twelve months fit visibly at enlarged text too, using compact axis labels. Both series retain year-labelled legends and unknown/partial markers.

## Artwork provenance and retained-data behavior

The new [flight-scene.svg](flight-scene.svg) is an original code-native decorative illustration created for this alternative, rasterized with CairoSVG to [flight-scene.png](../../../apps/platform-lab/assets/aviation/flight-scene.png) and the compact [flight-scene-detail.png](../../../apps/platform-lab/assets/aviation/flight-scene-detail.png). [FlightScene](../../../apps/platform-lab/src/mobile/FlightScene.tsx) renders the full header scene or 88 × 44 compact season art. No image model or external photograph was used for these assets. The illustration establishes no observed aircraft, location, weather or measured track. Original approved branding/reference images and licensed photographs remain preserved.

The shell retains Start and its processed selectors across ordinary tab navigation. Unchanged view inputs reuse summary/month calculations; returning to Start does not itself read the library. Duplicate native active notifications no longer trigger another read. Real background/foreground transitions and provider closure still perform guarded local revalidation; unavailable, changed-owner or failed reads do not publish cached data as verified. No persistent unscoped cache or generation-only shortcut was introduced. Host regressions cover these boundaries; they do not establish that the full user-reported reload symptom has been reproduced or eliminated on a device. Evidence and remaining gates belong in the [package report](../../verification/2026-10-22-alternative-ui.md) and [#45](https://github.com/MomoRC-tech/MoFlight-App-framework/issues/45).
