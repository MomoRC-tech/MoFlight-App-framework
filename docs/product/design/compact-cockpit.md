## Compact Flights screen — approved 24 October 2026

Manuel explicitly selected implementation of the full [#70](https://github.com/MomoRC-tech/MoFlight-App-framework/issues/70) screen proposal and its [refined C layout](proposals/2026-10-24-flight-cards-dense-c.html). This supersedes C09's earlier tall flight row and the scrolling search/summary header. A compact period/aircraft/search bar sits outside list scrolling. Selected controls and removable actual-value tags use teal with textual values and close marks; Reset appears only with active filters. Search opens a content-sized editor. Year/aircraft choices and actions wrap at their intrinsic sizes. The default is all selected-account saved flights; explicit dashboard period requests remain visible as filters.

Cards use 7 vertical / 10 horizontal padding, radius 10, 5 between rows and 2 between information lines. Date/registration/model share the first line, labelled pilot/Begleiter the second, and source start/route the third; reported h:min remains prominent at right. Content and system text can wrap, with no forced height, separators or repeated chevron. Names preserve supplied given names and abbreviate the family name for display; raw names remain in details. Extra companions/PIC/SIC stay labelled. Solo is shown only for an explicit saved `Solo`/`Alleinflug` flight-mode/type value with a recorded pilot and no contradictory additional crew; missing/blank crew alone never establishes it. No unverified source-code or legal-role mapping is added.

One compact line shows filtered/saved count, known reported time and any unknown-time count. Coverage, retrieval age and explanation move behind the information action; real recovery/error states remain visible. Detail/Back preserves list position and filters; account/store changes clear them. Phone and 600+ dp single-column lists cap at 480 dp; from 840 dp at ordinary text size, two columns fit within 960 dp. Verification and platform limits are recorded in the [package report](../../verification/2026-10-24-flight-list.md).
